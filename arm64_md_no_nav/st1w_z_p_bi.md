## ST1W (scalar plus immediate, single register)

Contiguous store words from vector (immediate index)

This instruction performs a contiguous store of words from elements of a vector register to the memory address generated by a 64-bit scalar base and immediate index in the range -8 to 7 that is multiplied by the vector's in-memory size, irrespective of predication, and added to the base address. Inactive elements are not written to memory.

The 128-bit element variant is illegal when executed in Streaming SVE mode, unless FEAT_SME_FA64 is implemented and enabled.

It has encodings from 2 classes: [32-bit and 64-bit elements](#iclass_32_bit_and_64_bit_elements) and [128-bit element](#iclass_128_bit_element)
