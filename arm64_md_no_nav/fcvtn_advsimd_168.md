## FCVTN (half-precision to 8-bit floating-point)

Half-precision convert to 8-bit floating-point (vector)

This instruction converts half-precision elements of the two source vectors to 8-bit floating-point while scaling the values by 2<sup>SInt(FPMR.NSCALE\[4:0\])</sup>, and places the in-order results in the 8-bit elements of the destination vector.

The 8-bit floating-point encoding format is selected by FPMR.F8D.
