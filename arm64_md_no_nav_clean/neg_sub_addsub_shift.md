## NEG (shifted register)

Negate (shifted register) This instruction negates an optionally-shifted register value, and writes the result to the destination register. This is an alias of [SUB (shifted register)](sub_addsub_shift.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [SUB (shifted register)](sub_addsub_shift.html). - The description of [SUB (shifted register)](sub_addsub_shift.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [SUB (shifted register)](sub_addsub_shift.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
