## LDNP

Load pair of registers, with non-temporal hint This instruction calculates an address from a base register value and an immediate offset, loads two 32-bit words or two 64-bit doublewords from memory, and writes them to two registers. For information about addressing modes, see Load/Store addressing modes. For information about non-temporal pair instructions, see Load/Store non-temporal pair.

#### Decode
