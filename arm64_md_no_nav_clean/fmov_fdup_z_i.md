## FMOV (immediate, unpredicated)

Move floating-point immediate to vector elements (unpredicated) This instruction unconditionally broadcasts the floating-point immediate into each element of the destination vector. This instruction is unpredicated. This is an alias of [FDUP](fdup_z_i.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [FDUP](fdup_z_i.html). - The description of [FDUP](fdup_z_i.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [FDUP](fdup_z_i.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
