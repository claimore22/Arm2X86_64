## SXTW

Sign extend word This instruction sign-extends a word to the size of the register, and writes the result to the destination register. This is an alias of [SBFM](sbfm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [SBFM](sbfm.html). - The description of [SBFM](sbfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [SBFM](sbfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
