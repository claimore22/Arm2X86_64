## FCVT

Floating-point convert (predicated) This instruction converts the size and precision of each active floating-point element of the source vector, and places the results in the corresponding elements of the destination vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified or are set to zero, depending on whether merging or zeroing predication is selected. Since the input and result types have a different size the smaller type is held unpacked in the least significant bits of elements of the larger size. When the input is the smaller type the upper bits of each source element are ignored. When the result is the smaller type the results are zero-extended to fill each destination element. It has encodings from 12 classes: [Half-precision to single-precision, merging](#iclass_half_precision_to_single_precision_merging) , [Half-precision to single-precision, zeroing](#iclass_half_precision_to_single_precision_zeroing) , [Half-precision to double-precision, merging](#iclass_half_precision_to_double_precision_merging) , [Half-precision to double-precision, zeroing](#iclass_half_precision_to_double_precision_zeroing) , [Single-precision to half-precision, merging](#iclass_single_precision_to_half_precision_merging) , [Single-precision to half-precision, zeroing](#iclass_single_precision_to_half_precision_zeroing) , [Single-precision to double-precision, merging](#iclass_single_precision_to_double_precision_merging) , [Single-precision to double-precision, zeroing](#iclass_single_precision_to_double_precision_zeroing) , [Double-precision to half-precision, merging](#iclass_double_precision_to_half_precision_merging) , [Double-precision to half-precision, zeroing](#iclass_double_precision_to_half_precision_zeroing) , [Double-precision to single-precision, merging](#iclass_double_precision_to_single_precision_merging) and [Double-precision to single-precision, zeroing](#iclass_double_precision_to_single_precision_zeroing)
