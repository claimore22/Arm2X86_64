## UBFIZ

Unsigned bitfield insert in zeros This instruction copies a bitfield of <width> bits from the least significant bits of the source register to bit position <lsb> of the destination register, setting the destination bits above and below the bitfield to zero. This is an alias of [UBFM](ubfm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [UBFM](ubfm.html). - The description of [UBFM](ubfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.
