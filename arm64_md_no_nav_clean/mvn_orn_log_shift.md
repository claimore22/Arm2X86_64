## MVN

Bitwise NOT This instruction writes the bitwise inverse of a register value to the destination register. This is an alias of [ORN (shifted register)](orn_log_shift.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [ORN (shifted register)](orn_log_shift.html). - The description of [ORN (shifted register)](orn_log_shift.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [ORN (shifted register)](orn_log_shift.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
