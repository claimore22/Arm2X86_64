## CMN (extended register)

Compare negative (extended register) This instruction adds a register value and a sign or zero-extended register value, followed by an optional left shift amount. The argument that is extended from the <Rm> register can be a byte, halfword, word, or doubleword. It updates the condition flags based on the result, and discards the result. This is an alias of [ADDS (extended register)](adds_addsub_ext.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [ADDS (extended register)](adds_addsub_ext.html). - The description of [ADDS (extended register)](adds_addsub_ext.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.
