## LD1R

Load one single-element structure and replicate to all lanes (of one register) This instruction loads a single-element structure from memory and replicates the structure to all the lanes of the SIMD&FP register. Depending on the settings in the CPACR_EL1, CPTR_EL2, and CPTR_EL3 registers, and the current Security state and Exception level, an attempt to execute the instruction might be trapped. It has encodings from 2 classes: [No offset](#iclass_no_offset) and [Post-index](#iclass_post_index)
