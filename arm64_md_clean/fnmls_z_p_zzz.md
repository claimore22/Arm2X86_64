## FNMLS

Floating-point negated fused multiply-subtract (predicated) This instruction multiplies the active floating-point elements of the first source vector by the corresponding floating-point elements of the second source vector, and subtracts the result from elements of the third source (addend) vector without intermediate rounding. The negated results are destructively placed in the destination and third source (addend) vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified.
