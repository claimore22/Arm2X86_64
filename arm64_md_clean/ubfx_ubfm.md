## UBFX

Unsigned bitfield extract This instruction copies a bitfield of <width> bits starting from bit position <lsb> in the source register to the least significant bits of the destination register, and sets destination bits above the bitfield to zero. This is an alias of [UBFM](ubfm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [UBFM](ubfm.html). - The description of [UBFM](ubfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.
