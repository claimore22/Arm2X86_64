## FCM\<cc\> (zero)

Floating-point compare with zero This instruction compares active floating-point elements in the source vector with zero, and places the boolean results of the specified comparison in the corresponding elements of the destination predicate. Inactive elements in the destination predicate register are set to zero. This instruction does not set the condition flags. | <cc> | Comparison            | |--------|-----------------------| | EQ     | equal                 | | GE     | greater than or equal | | GT     | greater than          | | LE     | less than or equal    | | LT     | less than             | | NE     | not equal             | | UO     | unordered             | It has encodings from 6 classes: [Equal](#iclass_equal) , [Greater than](#iclass_greater_than) , [Greater than or equal](#iclass_greater_than_or_equal) , [Less than](#iclass_less_than) , [Less than or equal](#iclass_less_than_or_equal) and [Not equal](#iclass_not_equal)
