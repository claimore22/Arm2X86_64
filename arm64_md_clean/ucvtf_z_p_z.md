## UCVTF (predicated)

Unsigned integer convert to floating-point (predicated) This instruction converts to floating-point from the unsigned integer in each active element of the source vector, and places the results in the corresponding elements of the destination vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified or are set to zero, depending on whether merging or zeroing predication is selected. If the input and result types have a different size the smaller type is held unpacked in the least significant bits of elements of the larger size. When the input is the smaller type the upper bits of each source element are ignored. When the result is the smaller type the results are zero-extended to fill each destination element. It has encodings from 14 classes: [16-bit to half-precision, merging](#iclass_16_bit_to_half_precision_merging) , [16-bit to half-precision, zeroing](#iclass_16_bit_to_half_precision_zeroing) , [32-bit to half-precision, merging](#iclass_32_bit_to_half_precision_merging) , [32-bit to half-precision, zeroing](#iclass_32_bit_to_half_precision_zeroing) , [32-bit to single-precision, merging](#iclass_32_bit_to_single_precision_merging) , [32-bit to single-precision, zeroing](#iclass_32_bit_to_single_precision_zeroing) , [32-bit to double-precision, merging](#iclass_32_bit_to_double_precision_merging) , [32-bit to double-precision, zeroing](#iclass_32_bit_to_double_precision_zeroing) , [64-bit to half-precision, merging](#iclass_64_bit_to_half_precision_merging) , [64-bit to half-precision, zeroing](#iclass_64_bit_to_half_precision_zeroing) , [64-bit to single-precision, merging](#iclass_64_bit_to_single_precision_merging) , [64-bit to single-precision, zeroing](#iclass_64_bit_to_single_precision_zeroing) , [64-bit to double-precision, merging](#iclass_64_bit_to_double_precision_merging) and [64-bit to double-precision, zeroing](#iclass_64_bit_to_double_precision_zeroing)
