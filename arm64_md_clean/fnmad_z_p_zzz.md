## FNMAD

Floating-point negated fused multiply-add to multiplicand (predicated) This instruction multiplies the active floating-point elements of the first source vector by the corresponding floating-point elements of the second source vector, and adds the result to elements of the third (addend) vector without intermediate rounding. The negated results are destructively placed in the destination and first source (multiplicand) vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified.
