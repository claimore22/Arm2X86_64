## FRINT\<r\>

Floating-point round to integral value (predicated) This instruction rounds to an integral floating-point value with the specified rounding option from each active floating-point element of the source vector, and places the results in the corresponding elements of the destination vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified or are set to zero, depending on whether merging or zeroing predication is selected. | <r> | Rounding Option                                | |-------|------------------------------------------------| | N     | to nearest, with ties to even                  | | A     | to nearest, with ties away from zero           | | M     | toward minus Infinity                          | | P     | toward plus Infinity                           | | Z     | toward zero                                    | | I     | current FPCR rounding mode                     | | X     | current FPCR rounding mode, signalling inexact | It has encodings from 14 classes: [Current mode signalling inexact, merging](#iclass_current_mode_signalling_inexact_merging) , [Current mode signalling inexact, zeroing](#iclass_current_mode_signalling_inexact_zeroing) , [Current mode, merging](#iclass_current_mode_merging) , [Current mode, zeroing](#iclass_current_mode_zeroing) , [Nearest with ties to away, merging](#iclass_nearest_with_ties_to_away_merging) , [Nearest with ties to away, zeroing](#iclass_nearest_with_ties_to_away_zeroing) , [Nearest with ties to even, merging](#iclass_nearest_with_ties_to_even_merging) , [Nearest with ties to even, zeroing](#iclass_nearest_with_ties_to_even_zeroing) , [Toward zero, merging](#iclass_toward_zero_merging) , [Toward zero, zeroing](#iclass_toward_zero_zeroing) , [Toward minus infinity, merging](#iclass_toward_minus_infinity_merging) , [Toward minus infinity, zeroing](#iclass_toward_minus_infinity_zeroing) , [Toward plus infinity, merging](#iclass_toward_plus_infinity_merging) and [Toward plus infinity, zeroing](#iclass_toward_plus_infinity_zeroing)
