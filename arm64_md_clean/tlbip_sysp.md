## TLBIP

TLB invalidate pair operation TLB invalidate pair operation. This is an alias of [SYSP](sysp.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [SYSP](sysp.html). - The description of [SYSP](sysp.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.
