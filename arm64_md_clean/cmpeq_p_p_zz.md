## CMP\<cc\> (vectors)

Compare vectors This instruction compares active integer elements in the first source vector with the corresponding elements in the second source vector, and places the boolean results of the specified comparison in the corresponding elements of the destination predicate. Inactive elements in the destination predicate register are set to zero. This instruction sets the First (N), None (Z), and !Last (C) condition flags based on the predicate result, and sets the V flag to zero. | <cc> | Comparison                   | |--------|------------------------------| | EQ     | equal                        | | GE     | signed greater than or equal | | GT     | signed greater than          | | HI     | unsigned higher than         | | HS     | unsigned higher than or same | | NE     | not equal                    | This instruction is used by the pseudo-instructions [CMPLE (vectors)](cmple_cmpeq_p_p_zz.html "Compare signed less than or equal to vector, setting the condition flags"), [CMPLO (vectors)](cmplo_cmpeq_p_p_zz.html "Compare unsigned lower than vector, setting the condition flags"), [CMPLS (vectors)](cmpls_cmpeq_p_p_zz.html "Compare unsigned lower than or same as vector, setting the condition flags"), and [CMPLT (vectors)](cmplt_cmpeq_p_p_zz.html "Compare signed less than vector, setting the condition flags"). It has encodings from 6 classes: [Equal](#iclass_equal) , [Greater than](#iclass_greater_than) , [Greater than or equal](#iclass_greater_than_or_equal) , [Higher](#iclass_higher) , [Higher or same](#iclass_higher_or_same) and [Not equal](#iclass_not_equal)
