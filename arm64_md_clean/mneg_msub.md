## MNEG

Multiply-negate This instruction multiplies two register values, negates the product, and writes the result to the destination register. This is an alias of [MSUB](msub.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [MSUB](msub.html). - The description of [MSUB](msub.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [MSUB](msub.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
