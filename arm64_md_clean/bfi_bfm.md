## BFI

Bitfield insert This instruction copies a bitfield of <width> bits from the least significant bits of the source register to bit position <lsb> of the destination register, leaving the other destination bits unchanged. This is an alias of [BFM](bfm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [BFM](bfm.html). - The description of [BFM](bfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [BFM](bfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
