## BFC

Bitfield clear This instruction sets a bitfield of <width> bits at bit position <lsb> of the destination register to zero, leaving the other destination bits unchanged. This is an alias of [BFM](bfm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [BFM](bfm.html). - The description of [BFM](bfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [BFM](bfm.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
