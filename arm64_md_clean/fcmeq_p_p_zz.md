## FCM\<cc\> (vectors)

Floating-point compare This instruction compares active floating-point elements in the first source vector with the corresponding elements in the second source vector, and places the boolean results of the specified comparison in the corresponding elements of the destination predicate. Inactive elements in the destination predicate register are set to zero. This instruction does not set the condition flags. | <cc> | Comparison            | |--------|-----------------------| | EQ     | equal                 | | GE     | greater than or equal | | GT     | greater than          | | NE     | not equal             | | UO     | unordered             | This instruction is used by the pseudo-instructions [FCMLE (vectors)](fcmle_fcmeq_p_p_zz.html "Floating-point compare less than or equal to vector"), and [FCMLT (vectors)](fcmlt_fcmeq_p_p_zz.html "Floating-point compare less than vector"). It has encodings from 5 classes: [Equal](#iclass_equal) , [Greater than](#iclass_greater_than) , [Greater than or equal](#iclass_greater_than_or_equal) , [Not equal](#iclass_not_equal) and [Unordered](#iclass_unordered)
