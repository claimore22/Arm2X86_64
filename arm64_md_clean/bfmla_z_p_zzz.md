## BFMLA (vectors)

BFloat16 fused multiply-add This instruction multiplies the active BFloat16 elements of the first source vector by the corresponding BFloat16 elements of the second source vector. The results are then added to elements of the third source (addend) vector without intermediate rounding and destructively placed in the destination and third source (addend) vector. Inactive elements in the destination vector register remain unmodified. This instruction follows SVE2 non-widening BFloat16 numerical behaviors. ID_AA64ZFR0_EL1.B16B16 indicates whether this instruction is implemented.
