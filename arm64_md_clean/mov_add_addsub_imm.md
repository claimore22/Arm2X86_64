## MOV (to/from SP)

Move register value to or from SP This instruction copies the value of a register to or from the stack pointer. This is an alias of [ADD (immediate)](add_addsub_imm.html). This means: - The encodings in this description are named to match the encodings of [ADD (immediate)](add_addsub_imm.html). - The description of [ADD (immediate)](add_addsub_imm.html) gives the operational pseudocode, any constrained unpredictable behavior, and any operational information for this instruction.

### Operation

The description of [ADD (immediate)](add_addsub_imm.html) gives the operational pseudocode for this instruction.
